# Baseline of today's engine, v2 answers (2026-11-19)

Phase 0 of light and fast: the baseline every later phase reports against. Times, COM-call counts and stage names
only, no figures from EPM. The tables below are the output of `scripts/bench_answers.py --rounds 3` (live, on the
owner's PC) as printed, without its per-run progress lines; it reads back the run-log lines (`config.RUN_LOG`) it
caused.

- Engine: smartass 0.2.26 (branch feat/light-fast-p0, with the run log of Task 1).
- Run: one warm-up answer (b01, target `new`) made one new workbook in the analyser folder; then 3 rounds of the
  9 test-set cases (rounds outer, cases inner) into that same workbook (target `session`). Medians over the ok
  rounds; the warm-up is not in the medians.
- Columns: `_total` is the whole tool call; `excel job` is the Excel job's own `_total`; COM calls are the
  counts of the job's top-level stages (open target, draw, save) summed, and only count calls made inside a stage.
- b05, b09 and b15 have no chart block, which `server._new_question` refuses (`guard_chart`). For them the bench
  runs `_new_question`'s own steps minus that one guard (the draw adds its implicit chart), still logged as
  fin_analyze; their rows say "(no chart block)". The case files are unchanged.
- The Excel the answers went into also held the workbook of an earlier bench run that day (19 answers), left open.
- Stages nest (`excel job.draw` holds `excel job.draw.answer`), so a stage's share and its parts' shares overlap;
  `drills.*` are summed over concurrent drills. `prepare.drill` runs (0.0 s) for every new question, also
  without a lines block.

## The three largest stages

The Excel job is 91% of all seconds (median 33.7 s of a 34.9 s answer), and its **draw** stage is the largest by far:
71% of all seconds, median 25.2 s per answer, made of the data sheet (5.7 s), the Sources sheet (4.4 s), the answer
sheet (4.2 s, of which page setup 1.7 s) and the charts (2.1 s). Second is **save** of the workbook (11%, median
3.9 s). Third is **prepare** (9%, median 1.6 s): the EPM fetch itself is small (prepare.fetch, median 1.3 s), and
b16's ledger drill adds 8.0 s there (prepare.drill on b16). Opening the target is 0.4 s. An answer makes 259 to
377 COM calls.

## Two effects to keep in mind when comparing later phases

- The workbook grows during the bench (1 to 28 answers), and the answers slow down with it: in the per-round table
  the cases without a lines block go from 17.2 s (round 1) to 28.2 s and 47.0 s, all of it in the Excel job (draw
  13.1 -> 34.2 s, save 1.5 -> 6.0 s), with the same COM calls (median 275.5) and a flat prepare (1.3-1.7 s). So
  each COM call gets slower as the workbook grows. The all-cases medians are therefore the middle of a workbook that
  grows from 1 to 28 answers; a later phase must bench the same way (warm-up plus 3 rounds of the 9 cases in one new
  workbook) to compare like for like.
- The warm-up (20.5 s) found Excel running and warm. b16's first drill included the drill browser's start (2.3 s)
  and sign-in (4.9 s), which a running server has warm; the b16 median hides it.
